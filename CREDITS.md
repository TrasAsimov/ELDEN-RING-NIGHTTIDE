# Credits & Permissions

NIGHTTIDE is a non-commercial fan project. This page records major inspirations and third-party work relevant to development. Credits will expand as integrations are finalized.

## Inspiration

### Elden Ring Ascended — vanbuinen

NIGHTTIDE originally began as a personal balance experiment inspired in part by **Elden Ring Ascended** and its large-scale, high-difficulty overhaul approach.

NIGHTTIDE is not an official continuation of Ascended. The public NIGHTTIDE project is being developed independently, and Ascended content is not being offered for redistribution through this public repository.

Special thanks to **vanbuinen** for creating Elden Ring Ascended and for the inspiration it provided during the project's early development.
