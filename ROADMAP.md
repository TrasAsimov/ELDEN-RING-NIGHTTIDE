# NIGHTTIDE Roadmap

This roadmap describes the current development direction. Priorities may change as testing exposes technical or balance issues.

## Phase 1 — Independent foundation

- Establish a clean NIGHTTIDE baseline independent from the historical Ascended-based development branch.
- Re-implement approved NIGHTTIDE balance targets on the clean base.
- Separate project-owned work from historical experiments and third-party source packages.
- Standardize English/Chinese naming and remove legacy project naming from the public-facing build.

## Phase 2 — Core balance

- Player HP / FP / stamina progression.
- Melee weapon reinforcement and scaling tiers.
- Talismans, armor set effects, skills, spells, consumables, and regeneration systems.
- Starting classes, memory slots, starting equipment, and keepsake selection.
- Enemy HP, damage, resistances, poise, posture, and reward tuning.
- Boss reward and progression systems.

## Phase 3 — Nightreign boss integration

Target: integrate selected Nightreign bosses as native-feeling encounters rather than simple replacements.

Current placement/design directions include:

- **Caligo** — Mountaintops of the Giants / Freezing Lake.
- **Maris** — an open area in Liurnia of the Lakes.
- **Heolstor** — the former Astel arena after the Lake of Rot route.
- **Gnoster + Faurtis** — integrate from an existing successful port and adapt to NIGHTTIDE.
- **Gladius** — Mt. Gelmir / Volcano region, arena still under evaluation.
- **Fulghor** — large open battlefield, final location still under evaluation.
- **Libra** — Abyssal Woods.
- **Adel** — Jagged Peak, using a sufficiently large encounter space.
- **Harmonia** — DLC late-game placement under evaluation.
- **Dreglord** — Caelid progression after Starscourge Radahn, with encounter structure adapted for NIGHTTIDE.

All placements remain subject to AI, navigation, event, camera, multiplayer, and performance testing.

## Phase 4 — World encounters and progression

- Rework selected field encounters and miniboss distribution.
- Add or relocate encounters where the world has suitable unused combat space.
- Improve encounter rewards and progression incentives.
- Preserve critical story progression and key items unless a redesign is intentional and tested.

## Phase 5 — Compatibility and polish

- English and Simplified Chinese text pass.
- Event / map / regulation conflict audit.
- Save/reload testing.
- Offline ModEngine compatibility.
- Boss AI, phase transitions, music, camera, lock-on, rewards, and death-event validation.
- Third-party credit and permission audit.
- Clean player package with no development-only files.

## Phase 6 — Public alpha

A public build will only be published after the standalone NIGHTTIDE package is sufficiently separated from historical private development content and passes basic runtime validation.

There is currently **no public download**.
